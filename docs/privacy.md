## What the app does
PresyoAlert PH shows reference prices of everyday food items in the Philippines, taken from public Department of Agriculture (DA) reports, and lets you keep a shopping list and your own price notes.

## Information we collect
**We do not collect personal information.** There are no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no location access.

## Information stored on your device
Your shopping list, your own price entries, and the downloaded price data are stored only on your phone. They are not sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

## Network use
The app downloads a public data file (`feed.json`) and product pictures from GitHub Pages (github.io). Like any web request, this lets GitHub see your IP address and basic request details under GitHub's own privacy statement. The app sends no personal data in these requests.

## Price data and accuracy
Prices come from DA reports and are averages for selected markets, not live prices at a specific store. They may be out of date or incomplete and are provided for general information only.

## Children
The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.
